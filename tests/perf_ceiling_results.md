## Reading it
- rAF is capped at 60 Hz here, so every frame is >8.3 ms and ~20–25% land just over 16.7 ms from timer jitter even at a solid 60 FPS; the >33 ms spike column and the lows are the useful ones. A 120 Hz phone can't be judged from this.
- In this proxy the cost is almost all raster (canvas pixels), not game JS: JS stays ~0.3–2.5 ms/frame for rs ≤ 1 even at CPU ×4–×6.
- Known issue found by the grid: **surface at rs 2.0 with fx ≥ 1 costs 12 ms JS at ×1 and 75–120 ms at ×4/×6** (fx 0 is fine). rs 2 is never used by default or by adapt (adapt only steps down), only by a manual `?rs=2`; not fixed in v8.
- `adapt=1` recovers a lot at CPU ×4 (deep rs 1 fx 1: 23 → 41 FPS) but adapt only steps every 0.75 s, so 5 s windows include the slow start.

## Recommended Galaxy S26 Ultra default (a guess from a proxy — not measured on the phone)
Keep the shipped defaults: **DPR cap 2 (`dprcap=2`), rs 1, fx 2, adapt on.** On a DPR ≈ 2.6 phone the cap means a canvas ratio of 2.0, which is the rs 0.75 row above (2.625 × 0.75 ≈ 1.97): 60 FPS at CPU ×1 in both scenes and 46–60 FPS at ×4, with adapt as the safety net (it drops fx, then rs down to 0.6). Do not raise the cap to the full device DPR (rs 1 rows: 20–40 FPS at ×4). Needs a real-phone check.
